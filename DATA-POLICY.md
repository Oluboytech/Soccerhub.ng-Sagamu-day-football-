# SoccerHub Sagamu Day Data Policy

This repository is intended for official SoccerHub competition data distribution.

Competition facts should be sourced from the Sagamu Youth Association and/or verified SoccerHub match coverage.

Images, logos, player photographs, and other media may have separate ownership or usage permissions. Do not assume that repository access grants unrestricted rights to every asset.

Never commit credentials, private personal information, or confidential registration documents.
